Privacy

TKJ Toolkit is designed so that your data stays on your device wherever technically possible. This page explains, honestly, what happens to the data you enter.

Client-side processing

The majority of tools — including all calculators, generators, encoders, formatters and converters — run entirely in your browser. The values you type are processed by JavaScript on your own device and are never transmitted to us or stored anywhere.

Tools that use public APIs

A small number of tools must contact an external service to work:

  • DNS Lookup sends the domain name you enter to Cloudflare's public DNS-over-HTTPS endpoint (cloudflare-dns.com) to resolve records. The domain you query is visible to that service.
  • HTTP Header Checker and HTTP Connectivity Check make requests directly from your browser to the URL you provide. The target server will see a normal request coming from your IP address.
  • QR Code Generator loads a small QR library from a CDN (cdnjs). Your QR content itself is rendered locally and not sent anywhere.

We do not operate these third-party services; their own privacy policies apply to the requests you send them.

Local storage

The site stores exactly one preference in your browser's local storage: your theme choice (light, dark or system). It contains no personal data and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels or advertising scripts.
  • No accounts, cookies or fingerprinting.
  • Generated passwords and MAC addresses are never logged or transmitted.

Because usage depends on where this site is hosted, we do not claim "100% private" operation — but by design there is no server-side component of TKJ Toolkit that receives your input.
