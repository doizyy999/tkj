CIDR Calculator

Parse CIDR notation like 192.168.1.10/24 into complete network details.

Format: IPv4 address followed by a slash and prefix length (0-32).

About this tool

The CIDR Calculator parses compact CIDR notation into every network property you need.

How it works

The notation is split into address and prefix, validated, and expanded into network, broadcast, mask, wildcard, host range and total hosts — all in your browser.
